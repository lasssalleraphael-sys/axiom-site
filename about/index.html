The process

From keystroke to finished task, three steps, one screen.

Summon

⌥Space anywhere in macOS. The pill expands into a single input. No app to open, no window to find.

Delegate

Say what you want done. Axiom picks the apps, plans the steps, and starts working across them.

Approve

Anything irreversible stops and asks. You see the step, you approve it, it runs. Then the pill returns to the edge of the screen.

Axiom

Axiom is a macOS agent that works across your apps instead of inside a chat tab. Built in Aix-en-Provence.

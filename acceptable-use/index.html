Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out what you may and may not do with Axiom — the macOS application, the agent that runs inside it, and the connectors it uses to reach your third-party accounts.

It applies to everyone who downloads or runs Axiom. By using the application, you agree to comply with it.

Prohibited Activities

You may not use Axiom to conduct, facilitate, or promote:

Illegal Activity: Anything violating French, European Union, or other applicable law, including fraud, unlicensed distribution of regulated goods, distribution of illegal material, or evasion of sanctions.

Malicious Cyber Operations: Distributing malware, running phishing or credential-harvesting campaigns, orchestrating denial-of-service attacks, or using Axiom's browser and connector access to probe systems you do not own.

Unauthorised Access: Connecting Axiom to accounts, inboxes, calendars, or files you do not own or have not been explicitly authorised to access. Axiom acts with the permissions you give it; giving it permissions that are not yours to give is a violation of this policy and likely of law.

Harassment and Abuse: Generating or distributing content that harasses, threatens, defames, or targets any individual or group.

Content Involving Minors: Any use involving the sexualisation or exploitation of minors. This results in immediate and permanent termination and referral to the appropriate authorities.

Automation Boundaries

Axiom runs autonomous multi-step tasks. That capability carries specific restrictions:

Deceptive Automation: You may not use Axiom to generate synthetic reviews, impersonate real people, run coordinated inauthentic messaging campaigns, or produce content designed to deceive markets, voters, or regulators.

Bulk Unsolicited Messaging: You may not use Axiom's mail or messaging connectors to send unsolicited bulk communications, or to circumvent the sending limits of the services you have connected.

Scraping in Violation of Terms: You may not configure Axiom to extract data from platforms in breach of those platforms' terms of service or robots.txt directives.

Circumventing Approval Gates: Axiom stops and asks before actions that send, delete, publish, or spend. You may not attempt to disable, automate around, or otherwise defeat that gate.

Fair Use of the Service

Plan Limits: Each tier includes a monthly interaction allowance and a per-task step ceiling. You may not use multiple accounts, automation, or any other method to circumvent these.

Infrastructure Load: You may not deliberately generate artificial task volume intended to degrade the service, exhaust rate limits, or inflate compute costs.

Reverse Engineering: You may not decompile, disassemble, or reverse-engineer the Axiom application to extract credentials, replicate its architecture, or build a competing product. Note that until an API proxy is in place, the beta build contains service credentials; extracting and using them is a violation of this policy and of the underlying providers' terms.

Security Testing: You may not run penetration tests, red-teaming scripts, or stress tests against Axiom's infrastructure without written authorisation. If you find a vulnerability, report it — see below.

Third-Party Provider Compliance

Axiom runs on foundational AI providers and connector infrastructure. Your use of Axiom must also comply with the usage policies of:

  • Google (Gemini API and Google Workspace services)

  • Composio (third-party application connections)

  • Any service you connect, including Notion, Slack, and others

If your use causes a provider to suspend or terminate access — whether ours or your own, on the Operator tier — the consequences and any resulting cost are your responsibility.

Enforcement

If we identify a violation, we may:

Suspend Access: Deactivate your account and revoke connector authorisations, without prior notice where the violation is serious or ongoing.

Terminate the Account: Permanently close your account for severe or repeated violations. Paid subscriptions terminated for cause are not refunded.

Report: Refer conduct to law enforcement or the affected third-party provider where the law requires it or the conduct warrants it.

We monitor aggregate usage patterns and error rates to keep the service running. We do not read the content of your tasks — see the Privacy Policy for what that means precisely.

Reporting a Violation or Vulnerability

To report abuse, a suspected security vulnerability, or a breach of this policy, contact supportAxiom26gmail.com.

If you are reporting a vulnerability in good faith, tell us before telling anyone else, give us a reasonable window to fix it, and we will not pursue you for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, zero-fluff technical and operational parameters regarding how we build, deploy, and secure your enterprise architectures.

"Axiom is damaged and can't be opened"

This is Gatekeeper, not a corrupted file. It appears because the app isn't signed by Apple yet. Follow step 4 — the xattr command clears it.

Which apps does it connect to?

Calendar, Mail, Drive, Notion, Slack and more, through a live catalogue that expands without an app update. Starter gets core connectors, Pro and Operator get everything.

Why isn't Axiom signed by Apple?

Signing requires an Apple Developer Program membership, which costs $99 a year. That's coming before public launch. During the beta the extra step is the trade-off for shipping earlier.

How is this different from Claude or ChatGPT?

Those are where you go to think. Axiom is where you go to hand something off. You can connect Claude or ChatGPT to your calendar and mail today — but you still open a window, describe your situation, and carry the result back out yourself. Axiom starts from the other end: it sits at the top of your screen inside the session you're already in, you press ⌥Space without leaving what you're doing, and it works across your apps in the background. Axiom also runs on Claude and Gemini underneath. It isn't a competitor to the model — it's the layer that puts one to work on your Mac.

What counts as an interaction?

One task, from your instruction to the final result — however many steps it takes internally.

The process

From keystroke to finished task, three steps, one screen.

Summon

⌥Space anywhere in macOS. The pill expands into a single input. No app to open, no window to find.

Delegate

Say what you want done. Axiom picks the apps, plans the steps, and starts working across them.

Approve

Anything irreversible stops and asks. You see the step, you approve it, it runs. Then the pill returns to the edge of the screen.
